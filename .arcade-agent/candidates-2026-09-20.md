## A. Prism Warden — The Drowned Beacons (SELECTED)

Fantasy: a lighthouse keeper crosses a drowned kingdom carrying its last mirror
shield. Classic Zelda-style action adventure with new IP. Moment to moment:
position, aim reflected sunlight, parry a projectile, slash an exposed guardian,
and decide whether to detour to restore a safer route. The twist is that the
same aimed reflection powers architecture and turns enemy fire into ammunition;
a raised shield slows movement, so routing light exposes you to flanking attacks.

Full inventory: five connected authored regions with five major challenges each
(25 total), five regional guardians, three new optical traversal tools, four
mutually exclusive equipment choices, two optional consequential discoveries per
region, checkpointed regional progression, a named cast and ending. Region themes:
tidal abbey, canopy aqueduct, glass kiln, night observatory, drowned crown. The
finale reconfigures the lighthouse while fighting a three-phase eclipse keeper.
First playable: one complete abbey encounter using beam redirection, sword,
dodge, projectile return, an optional sanctuary circuit and a rescue payoff.

Ironwake comparison: 5 regions >= 5 chapters; 25 authored challenges >= 20
objectives; reflection/combat/route changes interlock; equipment changes verbs
rather than only numbers; ten optional discoveries affect routes/guardians;
three-phase finale and a story ending. All except first playable are planned.

## B. Whiteout Relay — alpine rescue action

Fantasy: a rescue snowboarder outruns an avalanche to recover a mountain team.
Moment action: carve, brake, jump, land, tow a casualty, select a rescue route.
Twist: towing changes turning radius and jump timing; deliberately cut snow
shelves to divert avalanche flow. Meaningful challenge is line choice and safe
extraction, not a racing reskin. Set-piece: cross a collapsing cable-car span
with a rescued passenger. First playable: one slope, two approaches and rescue.
Full game: five authored mountains x five distinct rescue/set-piece courses,
four equipment tradeoffs (tow handling, wind sail, ice edges, anchors), alternate
weather routes, ten optional radio beacons and a summit-to-valley evacuation
finale with a recovered-team ending. Five regions/25 challenges meet Ironwake's
breadth; evolving traversal/rescue systems, loadout tradeoffs, secrets and
multi-stage climax meet the planned systems floor. Distinct from Zephyr's closed
kart circuits but some catalog movement overlap; rejected for this cycle.

## C. Ember Dispatch — tactical firefighting platform adventure

Fantasy: fire marshal saves a city aboard a runaway furnace train. Action:
aim hose, cool metal, breach doors, vent smoke, carry survivors and reroute water.
Twist: steam pressure opens alternate routes but also drives machinery hazards.
Set-piece: reverse a collapsing engine's turbines while evacuating the carriage.
First playable: one burning station where a pressure bypass enables a rescue.
Full inventory: five authored districts/train sections x five challenges,
five distinct emergencies, four nozzle/tool loadout choices, ten optional
rescue/supply branches, persistent saved crews assisting later stages, checkpointed
story and a three-stage reactor shutdown/escape ending. Equivalent or greater
than Ironwake: 5/25 breadth, interacting water/heat/pressure/rescue systems,
consequential crew progression and routing, climax. Distinct from combat games,
but fire-spread readability and simulation are higher first-cycle risks.

## Decision

Choose A for the strongest immediately testable skill interaction and a catalog
gap: deliberate exploration/action with an aimed defensive tool. It follows the
80% classic-foundation direction while its optical combat/architecture interaction
adds novelty. B and C meet the written scope floor but are not active projects.
No pitch gets credit for unimplemented scope, fun, or AAA presentation.
