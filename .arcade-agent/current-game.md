**Blastwick** — a classic Bomberman-lineage arena maze game with a new twist:
every bomb is a *fuse line*. Lit bombs ignite neighbouring bombs and the fuse
colour decides the blast pattern (cross, diagonal, ring), so the score comes from
planning chained detonations across a destructible grid while the arena floor
slowly floods/collapses from the edges. Waves of wick-sprites, power-ups, a boss
every fifth stage; keyboard/touch; numeric score for the leaderboard.

`claude/beautiful-turing-m5szz3`. Previous game: prism-warden (complete).
