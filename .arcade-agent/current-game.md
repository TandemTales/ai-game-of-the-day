**Wickfire** — a Bomberman-style grid-arena blaster (classic SNES/arcade lineage, new IP).
You are a foundry blaster clearing sealed ore vaults. The twist: there are no bomb timers.
You pay out a burning-ready *wick* behind you as you walk; charges sit on the wick and
detonate only when fire runs down it. Lure Sootlings across your wick, step clear, light it,
and chain-detonate everything in one go. Endless levels, score-attack leaderboard.
