Pitch: Hedgeshift is a classic Pac-Man-style maze chase (new IP) about a
firefly eating dewdrops in a moonlit hedge maze while four distinct shadow
wisps hunt it. Twist: the hedge maze has two wall layers, Day and Dusk. Every
~14 seconds the hedge shifts to the other layer (telegraphed by a 2s tremble),
opening new corridors and sealing others, and crushing/respawning wisps caught
in a closing wall. Moon lanterns (power pellets) let you scare wisps.

Note: no `dev` branch exists in this repo; nightly work happens on the
designated session branch and is promoted to main at release.
