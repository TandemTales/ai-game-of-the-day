**Lantern Moth** - a classic maze-chase (Pac-Man lineage, original IP). You are a moth
gliding through a night-hedge maze, eating firefly seeds while four spiders hunt you.
Big lanterns let you blind and scare spiders. Twist: living hedge gates swing open and
shut on a telegraphed rhythm, so the maze itself reshapes every few seconds and
escape routes you counted on can seal behind you.
