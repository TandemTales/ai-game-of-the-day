# Replacement proposals — Ironwake selected

Proposed 2026-09-07 after the human abandoned Orbit Orchard. The human selected Ironwake and authorized building it. Skybreak Squadron and
Ashen Crown remain unselected alternatives. See current-game.md for active state.

1. **Ironwake — 3D mech demolition (Three.js).** Pilot a heavy salvage mech
   through occupied city blocks. Rip out structural supports, topple towers into
   armored convoys, and tear weapons off enemy machines. Every collapse changes
   cover and routes. Heat forces a choice between sustained fire and exposing
   the reactor to vent. Target: three dense districts and a moving fortress boss;
   first playable: one city block where an aimed building collapse destroys a
   convoy. Score rewards chained destruction, objectives and speed. Distinct
   from the existing side-scrolling brawler and base builders through targeted
   structural destruction and city-scale third-person combat. Lead recommendation.

2. **Skybreak Squadron — 3D flight action (Three.js).** A mission-based flight
   campaign through storm canyons, burning shipyards and the interior of a giant
   carrier. Banking, boost/brake and lock-on attacks lead into multi-stage bosses.
   A near-miss maneuver charges a short time-slow burst: earn precision by flying
   dangerously. Branching routes hide ace encounters and score opportunities.
   Target: three authored missions; first playable: canyon pursuit into a capital
   ship fight. Distinct from existing kart racing and top-down arena shooters.
   Classic flight-action reference: Star Fox 64 3D (Nintendo).
   https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-3DS-games/Star-Fox-64-3D-274875.html

3. **Ashen Crown — illustrated 2D boss rush.** Duel enormous cursed monarchs
   in painted, animated arenas using generated character/environment art and
   deliberately authored attack animations. Dodge, parry and break armor; each
   defeated boss grants one signature move to equip for the next fight. Later
   phases break the arena and change safe ground. Target: five distinct bosses;
   first playable: one complete three-phase duel that rewards reading tells over
   button mashing. Score rewards clean counters, clear time and damage avoided.
   Distinct from the existing scrolling brawler and wave survival games through
   authored one-on-one duels. Combat reference: Furi (The Game Bakers).
   https://www.thegamebakers.com/furi/

All scope targets are proposals, not promises of completed content. All support
keyboard/mouse and touch; tune mobile controls as part of the first playable.
