Crimson Descent

Feather your thrusters and guide the expedition lander onto the Phobos Prospect landing pad. High atmosphere winds will punish rough touchdowns, so keep your velocity and tilt in check.

Ready? Tap, click, or press Space to ignite and begin descent.

Controls · Arrow/WASD to rotate · Space/Up to fire main thruster · On-screen buttons available on touch devices.

Mission Status

Use the buttons above to retry or review the mission rankings.

Mission Briefing

  • Maintain vertical speed under 35 and horizontal drift under 20 when touching down.
  • Keep your tilt within 20° as you settle onto the pad.
  • Saving fuel earns big bonuses. Fire thrusters only when you must.
  • Score is based on touchdown precision, remaining fuel, and time spent in descent.
  • Cratering ends the run, but you can still submit your score.

Controls

  • Rotate: Arrow Left/Right or A/D
  • Main Thruster: Arrow Up, W, or Spacebar
  • Touch: Use the on-screen buttons for rotate and thrust
