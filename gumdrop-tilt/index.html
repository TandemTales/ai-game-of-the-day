GUMDROP TILT

Drop pairs of gumdrops. Touch four of a colour to pop them; pops that drop more matches make chains. Every 8 drops the whole jar tilts and the stack slides sideways — chains born from a tilt pay double. The arrow shows which way it will lean.

←→ move · Z/X or ↑ rotate · ↓ soft drop · Space hard drop · P pause. Touch: swipe to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop.
