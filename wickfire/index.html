WICKFIRE

Lay a fuse. Light the mine.

  • Move arrows / WASD / left stick
  • Bomb Space / BOMB button
  • Cut fuse X / Shift / CUT button
  • You trail a live fuse. Any blast on it burns along it, detonating every bomb threaded on it — and burning you at its end. Cut it to escape, then ignite it from afar.
